Questions for artists

	3D:
		1: "What software do you prefer to use?"
		2: "What would you describe your visual style as? For example: stylised, or realistic, or a mix?"
		2.5: "What do you think you can bring to the team?"
		3: "Describe to me the process for modelling an asset?"
		4: "What's your experience in UV unwrapping?"
		5: "How would you optimise a 3D model we give you?"
		6: "How do you ensure an object is suitable for real-time rendering?" (Skippable)
		7: *"What were some challenging 3D models you've done, and how did you overcome them?" (Skippable)

	2D:
		1: "What software do you prefer to use?"
		2: "What's the biggest inspiration in your art?"
		3: "How do you approach doing colour theory for a concept art piece?" (Skippable)
		4: "How do you handle feedback from clients or directors on your work?" (Skippable)
		5: "If I gave you the prompt 'illustrate concept art for a mouse bartender', what would be your workflow to achieve this?"
 		6: "What were some challenging art pieces you've done, and how did you overcome them?" (Skippable)

	Design:
		1: "What kind of levels do you like to do?"
		2: "What are you favourite games?" (Skippable)
		3: "What do you think makes good level design?"
		4: "What’s your process when designing a level from scratch?"
		5: "Say if we were making a first-person, tactical shooter, with stealth as the main mechanic: what would be your process to design the level?"
		6: "Walk me through your favourite piece in your portfolio — why does it stand out to you?"
		7: "Our game takes place in different parts of a house — how would you approach designing levels in that kind of space?"
		8: "What was the core idea behind this level? - If you were to revisit it today, what would you change?"
		9: "What’s something you’re currently trying to improve or learn as a designer?"
